## Want to be listed?

If you or your organization use DevPaper in public documentation, blog posts, developer tooling, or research papers, we would love to feature your project!

Please [submit the "Add my project" form](https://github.com/DevPaper/devpaper/issues/new?template=used_by.yml).

### Listing Criteria:
- Factual and modest entries only.
- The project or repository must be publicly accessible and verifiable.
- Must include the version or commit tag used.
- Do not submit private client names or claim commercial endorsement.
- You may ask maintainers to update or remove an entry at any time by opening an issue.
